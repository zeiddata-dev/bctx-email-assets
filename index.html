Gmail

Gmail on a computer

Chrome, Edge, or Safari. Takes about two minutes.

  1. Click Copy signature 1 above.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon top right, then See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature.
  4. Click Create new, name it New, click Create.
  5. Click into the big empty box and paste with Ctrl+V. The signature appears with the photo and logo.
  6. Come back here, click Copy signature 3.
  7. Click Create new again, name it Reply, paste into its box.
  8. Just below, under Signature defaults: set FOR NEW EMAILS USE to New, and ON REPLY/FORWARD USE to Reply.
  9. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes. Easy to miss. Nothing saves without it.
After pasting, do not touch Gmail's formatting toolbar. The B, italics, font and color buttons will rewrite the layout and break the spacing.

Outlook

Outlook on a computer

The desktop app on Windows.

  1. Click Copy signature 1 above.
  2. In Outlook, open a New Email.
  3. In the ribbon at the top, click Signature, then Signatures... in the dropdown.
  4. Click New, name it New, click OK.
  5. Click into the Edit signature box at the bottom and paste with Ctrl+V.
  6. Come back here, click Copy signature 3. Click New again, name it Reply, paste it in.
  7. Top right, under Choose default signature: set New messages to New and Replies/forwards to Reply.
  8. Click OK.
If the layout looks cramped in Outlook, that is Outlook, not the signature. It renders correctly for everyone receiving it.

Outlook in a browser

outlook.com or Microsoft 365 on the web.

  1. Click Copy signature 1 above.
  2. Click the gear icon top right, then Mail on the left, then Compose and reply.
  3. Under Email signature, click + New signature, name it New, paste into the box.
  4. Click + New signature again, name it Reply, and paste signature 3 into that one.
  5. Under Select default signatures: set For new messages to New and For replies/forwards to Reply.
  6. Click Save.

Check it worked

  1. Send yourself a test email. The photo and logo should appear, not empty boxes.
  2. Reply to that email. The short signature should appear instead of the full one.
  3. Click the phone number on your phone. It should start a call.
Anything looks wrong, send a screenshot rather than trying to fix it in the settings box.
